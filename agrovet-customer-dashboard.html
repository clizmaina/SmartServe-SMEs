Farming Advice

🌽 Maize Growing Tips

Plant during the onset of long rains. Use certified hybrid seeds and apply DAP fertiliser at planting. Top-dress with CAN at knee height.

🐄 Dairy Cattle Care

Deworm every 3 months. Vaccinate against FMD and Lumpy Skin Disease. Provide clean water and balanced dairy meal for optimal milk production.
