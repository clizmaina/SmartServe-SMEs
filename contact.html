Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to register?

Customers sign up completely free. Business providers pay KSh 300 per month — that's less than KSh 10 per day.

How do I receive payments?

Payments are collected via M-Pesa directly to your till number. No bank account required.

Do I need a computer to use SmartServe?

No — the platform works on any smartphone browser. No app download needed.

Can I manage multiple business types?

Yes — you can create separate accounts for different business types using the same email address.

Is my customer data safe?

Yes. We use secure authentication, encrypted sessions and never share your data with third parties.

How do I get started?

Click "Get Started", choose your business type, sign up with your email and pay via M-Pesa. Takes under 5 minutes.
